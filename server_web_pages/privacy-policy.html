Privacy Policy

NOVA X Browser · developed by Tech Lyfe · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information NOVA X Browser ("NOVA X", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Account information

If you create a NOVA X account, we collect the username and email address you provide. These are used to create and secure your account and to sign you in.

Business listings

If you list a business in NOVA X Business, we store the details you submit: business name, description, category, location, website and any image you upload. Business listings you publish are visible to other users of the app.

Rewards data

If you use the rewards features, we store your points balance, daily streaks, and feature unlocks so the program works across sessions and devices.

Push notification token

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications and announcements. This involves a device push token managed by Google Firebase. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to our servers:

Browsing & search history Local bookmarks Downloads Saved passwords Screenshots & screen recordings App preferences

You can clear this data at any time from within the app or by clearing the app's storage.

2. Device permissions

NOVA X requests certain permissions only to provide specific features:

  • Internet & network state — to load web pages and connect to our services.
  • Camera & microphone — when a website you visit requests them (NOVA X asks you first), when you speak to the NOVA assistant, or when you choose to record your screen with sound. NOVA X never records on its own.
  • Screen capture — only when you take a screenshot of your whole screen or record your screen. Android shows its own permission dialog every time, and a notification stays visible while recording. Screenshots and recordings are saved only on your phone and are never uploaded by NOVA X.
  • Display over other apps (optional) — only if you turn on floating recording controls; used to show a small Pause/Stop control while you record in other apps.
  • Biometric / fingerprint — used locally to lock and unlock protected areas of the app. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, secure and operate your account.
  • To publish and display business listings you choose to list.
  • To operate the rewards program.
  • To send notifications and announcements you have not opted out of.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services

NOVA X relies on the following service providers to function:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging) — push notifications. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Secure cloud hosting — storage and processing for NOVA X services, including uploaded business images.
  • Web content — when you browse, the websites you visit are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time — from inside the app (Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Account) or via our account-deletion page:

Delete your account & data

When you delete your account, your profile, business listings, uploaded images, bookmarks synced to the account, rewards data, support tickets and push tokens are permanently removed from our servers. For security and fraud prevention, a short record of the deleted account (account name, email address, deletion date and recent account activity) is kept for up to 12 months and then erased automatically; payment records are kept only as long as the law requires.

6. Children's privacy

NOVA X is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at etech847@gmail.com.
